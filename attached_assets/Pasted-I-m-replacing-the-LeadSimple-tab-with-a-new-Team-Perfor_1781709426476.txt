## Summary of what this does:

- Removes the "LeadSimple" tab entirely
- Adds a "Team Performance" tab between Dashboard and CEO View
- The new tab has a role selector (6 roles: PM, PA, BK, LS, MK, AA)
- Each role shows its accountability KPIs in a scored table with:
  - KPI name with source badges (BD, RE, LS)
  - Target vs Actual values
  - Good/Better/Best score (0-3)
  - Dollar payout per KPI
- A bonus calculator bar shows the total quarterly bonus estimate
- Marketing Specialist shows as a placeholder (hire pending, no KPIs yet)
- The scoring engine auto-calculates: Best (3) = met target, Better (2) = within 10%, Good (1) = within 20%, Red (0) = missed by >20%
